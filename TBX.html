SUPPORT / FAQ

Good to
know.

Every TBX hoodie is cut oversized and boxy with dropped shoulders. If you want a closer fit, size down one. The fabric is a heavyweight 450 GSM brushed fleece that softens with every wash.

Orders are packed and shipped within 2 business days. Standard shipping is $5.99 and arrives in 5–7 business days. You'll get a tracking link by email the moment it leaves the studio.

Unworn pieces with tags can be returned within 14 days for a full refund. Drops are limited runs, so exchanges depend on remaining stock — reach out fast and we'll sort you out.

Machine wash cold, inside out, with like colors. Hang or lay flat to dry to preserve the embroidery and the oversized cut. Skip the dryer and it'll outlast everything else in your rotation.

Rarely in the same form. TBX works in limited drops — when a tone is gone, it's usually gone. Join the newsletter or follow @tbx.takeaction for first access to the next release.
